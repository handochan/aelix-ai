## 3. Corrections to the 2026-08-01 plan (measured, carry these forward)

1. **Track S "session flock" is the wrong fix.** The two-terminal loss is not torn bytes (O_APPEND
   already prevents that) — `append_entry` reparents onto a **process-local** `_current_leaf_id`, so the
   second writer's turn becomes an invisible orphan branch. Real fix = exclusive single-writer lock or
   re-read-tail-before-append: a design decision (with a #46 Windows leg), sized **L not M**. Track S
   realistically **~2 weeks**, not the audit's ~1.
2. **Windows full support is 5–7 weeks, not 4–6** (suite growth + the new delegation spawn surface).
   A "works, marked experimental, CI at continue-on-error" slice is **2–3 weeks**.
3. **pyright no-op root-caused:** pyright does not expand the `packages/*/src` glob segment in
   `include` (isolated repro) — enumerate four literal paths. 35 untriaged errors behind it.
4. **RUF100 is not a lint flip:** 384/384 findings are `non-enabled` — annotations for rulesets never
   selected (BLE001 ×278…). It is a ruleset-adoption decision; `--fix` would delete 384 intent comments.
5. **`dist-reservation/` is a live foot-gun:** the on-disk wheels still carry the pre-fix **stable
   0.0.0** that would permanently shadow the beta. Whoever runs Gate 0 must `rm -rf` and rebuild first.
6. **#91 shipped two undocumented surfaces:** `extension verify` missing from top-level `--help`
   (present in `extension --help`), and `--trust-extension-path` + the editable-install manifest
   downgrade have zero doc coverage — a new bar-(b)/(e) gap created after the audit.
