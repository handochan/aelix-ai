## Deviations from the design, with reasons

1. **Chain header time is the SUM of the steps' ages, not the max** (D4 said
   "max elapsed"). Measured live: the max read `9s` on a chain call 15 s old,
   because the steps run one after another. Batches keep the max.
2. **Task labels are cut in the MIDDLE from 24 cells** (D4: "cut with `…`").
   A fan-out's tasks differ at their end; an end cut turns four rows into one
   string (ADR-0227 measured exactly that). Below 24 cells the cut is at the end.
3. **Stable columns.** The two-row layout's numbers share one right-hand
   column (from every member's numbers), and the compact layout's label column
   is sized from the labels and the width only. Without this a label was re-cut
   whenever its own row's elapsed time gained a digit, or a member started a
   call — visible jitter. ~~Labels still re-cut, all at once, when the widest
   number gains a digit (parallel 60x30 below, at 10 s).~~ *Review round 1
   (F9): the shared column is sized for every elapsed time and token count at
   five cells (`1m00s`, `12.3k`) while that leaves the labels their whole width
   or the 24 cells a middle cut needs, so labels keep their cut through
   9s→10s→1m00s and 99→0.1k; at least two cells separate a label from its
   numbers in every layout.*
4. **An agreeing batch's model is shown whole or not at all** (<= 32 cells,
   only beside the counts and the elapsed time); no row takes it back. D4 only
   says "the batch model when the members agree"; ADR-0227's old fallback (rows
   take a dropped model back) belonged to the job-label layout and is recorded
   as superseded with its cost (ADR-0278 Limits).
5. **Narrow headers shed terms in an order**: ~~numbers first, then the model
   (N=1, decided with the widest state word so it does not flicker between
   running/thinking), then the profile (N>=2, before the counts are cut).~~
   *Review round 1 (F7) — what the code does now, in both headers: the cost,
   the idle time, the tokens, then the model, then the profile, and the
   elapsed time LAST (it is what the 1 s tick moves); a batch's counts are
   never dropped. The model and the profile are judged with the elapsed time
   at five cells and the widest state word, so the left side does not shift
   when the clock gains a digit; a number is judged at the width it has now.
   The order is strict (a header too narrow for the model shows no tokens
   either).
   Before, at 56–61 columns the one-child header kept
   `anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5` and dropped the time.*
6. **The Esc frame reads a member as stopped when its envelope says
   `aborted`**, whatever its last snapshot says. Found in the wiring test: a
   channel that raises the cancel without marking its row leaves `_run` to
   promote it to `error`, which drew `✗ failed — the turn was cancelled`.
   (PrintChannel marks `stopped` itself; the rule is defensive.)
7. **The Esc frame's time is `_format_elapsed` (`6s`)**, not the mockup's
   `3.6s`, to keep D4's one time format; `N turns` comes from the envelope's
   `usage.turns`. In the N=1 Esc frame the third row is the changed files, else
   the last reply, else blank. *Review round 1 (F8): the last row offers a
   resume only when the envelope has a `session_id`; otherwise it reads "It
   has no session, so it cannot be resumed." (a batch: `· no session to
   resume`).*
8. **No frame for a call none of whose members published** (`linger` returns
   False and the group ends). D7's `○ … not started` is kept for members of a
   call where some started.
9. **`begin_group` draws nothing**; the first frame comes with the first
   snapshot (milliseconds later — `_run` publishes before its first await), so a
   call refused inside `_run` does not flash a panel. The height is still fixed
   at `begin_group`.
10. **Row tokens under 100 are bare digits** (`15`), `0.4k` from 100 — the
    scripted provider's 15-token contexts show `3s · 15`; a real child's
    context is thousands of tokens.
11. **`_report_cancelled` gained an optional `snapshot` argument** so the
    report and the frame are built from one `CallEnvelopes.snapshot()`.

No change to `chrome.py`, `shell.py` or `render.py` was needed. `tui/commands.py`,

## Open risks for the reviewer

- `●`/`■`/`▶`/`○` are East Asian ambiguous width; a terminal drawing them
  wide would wrap a full-width row (the chrome's own `●` shares this).
- The panel's widget slot also holds the streaming tail (`__stream__`);
  slot order is dict insertion order, unchanged here.
- The Esc frame stays through slash commands; only a prompt (or a new
  delegation, or a session switch) takes it down.
- `grep` shows its path, not its pattern, when it has both (trail preference).
- Rendering cost: one `format_panel` per 250 ms per open call, plus 1 Hz;
  `_fit_label` flattens each task (<= 8 192 chars) per frame.

### Deviations from the fix list, with reasons

1. **The five-call live run uses `grep` on a FIFO, not `bash sleep 6`.**
   `bash` is `execution_mode="sequential"`, and one sequential tool makes the
   kernel run the whole batch sequentially (`aelix_agent_core/loop.py:711-718`), so with
   `bash` first the four reads cannot finish while it is open — measured:
   `fivecalls-bash-80x30` shows `▶ $ sleep 6` for 6 s and the reads only
   after it. `grep` and `read` are parallel tools; ripgrep blocks reading a
   FIFO until the driver writes to it 7 s later, which is the cross-review's
   slow-`grep` shape. Both runs are below.
2. **pyte cannot draw ZWJ or VS16.** Its `Screen.draw` stops a write at a
   zero-width character that is not a combining mark (MEASURED: `"a❤️b"`
   paints `a❤`, `"a👨‍👩b"` paints `a👨`). The live driver drops U+200D
   and U+FE0F before pyte (prompt_toolkit gives both 0 cells, so its column
   arithmetic is unchanged), and the glass test uses regional indicators,
   skin tones and flags only; ZWJ and `❤️` are pinned by the unit tests.
3. **The reserved numbers column yields to a middle cut.** Reserving five
   cells at 40 columns turned every label into an end cut
   (`[TAG:A] Read note…`); the reservation applies only while the labels keep
   their full width or 24 cells.
4. **The header's numbers are judged at their width now, its left parts at
   the reserve** (the first draft reserved every number, which hid `15 tok`
   on a 60-column batch header that had room for it).
5. **Cost:** `perf.py` worst case (8 members, 64 KiB tasks of combining
   marks) 11.1 → 16.9 ms per frame; ascii 5.3 → 5.7, CJK 10.3 → 9.7 (labels
   are now flattened once per frame — `/tmp/132-fix-live/identity.py`: 672
   frames identical before/after that refactor).

### Deviations from the fix list, with reasons

1. **G1 is decided per label, not for the batch.** The fix list's rule
   switches every label to the current width when any label would go from
   whole to cut. Then a label cut anyway is cut at the current width and
   re-cut at 9 s → 10 s → 1m00s whenever a neighbour is whole only there —
   F9's instability back. Per label: a label whole at the current width is
   whole; any other is cut at the reserve. Where no label is
   whole-only-now, the two rules give the same frame; where one is, mine
   keeps the others' cut. The lead's rule as a mutant
   (`g1_lead_literal_rule`) fails
   `test_a_cut_label_keeps_its_cut_while_a_neighbour_is_whole`.
2. **G2 judges the tokens at `12.3k tok` and lets an empty idle/tokens term
   hold its slot.** "Each optional term at a stable width" read literally:
   with the tokens at their width now, `99 tok` → `0.1k tok` can drop the
   tokens and let the idle time or the cost in; with no slot, the idle time
   appearing after five quiet seconds pushes the cost out and back. The
   price, measured (`header_diffs.py`, 20 000 random headers against
   `6d4c13b6`): a live one-child header shows fewer numbers in 357 and more
   in 203; without the tokens reserve 218 fewer, without the idle reserve
   146. The tokens reserve is one line (`_TOKENS_RESERVE`) and one test
   (`test_no_header_term_comes_and_goes_as_the_tokens_grow`) if the lead
   prefers the tokens at their width. Two consequences seen live: at 60x30
   the batch header shows `· 15 tok` until a member finishes and the counts
   widen to `2 running · 1 done`, then not (round 1 kept it by judging the
   tokens beside the elapsed time's actual width — the thing G2 rules out;
   the counts have no slack); and at 24–26 columns a one-child header shows
   no elapsed time (round 1's came and went with the clock there).
3. **G3's "fixed phrases" are `— the turn was cancelled`, both hints (header
   and the one-child row) and `· no result`.** `· changed:` is never cut
   (the file names after it are), and `not started` is a member's state, not
   an appended phrase. Dropping the one-child hint row leaves the gutter
   alone below 51 columns (47 for the no-session hint).
4. **G4 raises the window to 256 instead of making the guarantee
   unconditional.** No bounded record can name an open call in every stream:
   naming one needs its id, and a child can open more calls than any bound
   and end the ones kept first. So the bound sits where a model's parallel
   batch does not reach it — 252 calls open at once (256 with no finished
   call kept) — and ADR Limits says what happens past it. Memory per entry:
   a name and an argument of at most 120 characters and the call id; the
   first 200 entries are the trail's own objects.
5. **G5 caches the flattened labels** (`_flat_label`, 16 entries — two full
   batches; a task is at most 64 KiB of UTF-8, at most 256 KiB as a `str`),
   because flattening eight 64 KiB tasks was 10 of the 13 ms left after the
   bisection, paid on every redraw and every 1 s tick. The first frame of a
   group still pays it (13.6 ms worst); the label path is checked identical
   (9 000 labels).
6. **Two regressions in this round's own first draft, found before the
   commit and fixed.** (a) `_now`'s new `room = …` for `· no result` rebound
   the parameter its `left()` closure reads, so the files a stopped member
   changed got 1 cell — the live Esc frame at 60 columns read `· changed: …`
   (the first live batch; `superseded-r2a/`). Now `call`, with a guard test.
   (b) The call's room was measured before the `■ ` mark instead of after, so
   every cut stopped call got two more cells than round 1 gave it — found by
   `diff_classify.py` (705 rows outside the intended categories), fixed to
   round 1's arithmetic, pinned by
   `test_a_stopped_call_is_cut_where_round_1_cut_it`. A third, (c), was the
   Esc header's frozen elapsed time judged at its reserve, which hid `2 turns`
   at 66 columns (found by `g3_check.py`); its numbers now have no reserve.
7. **perf3.py's eight tasks are one string eight times**, so after the label
   cache its n=8 figures are warm frames; `perf4.py` gives each member a
   distinct task and measures the first frame (cache cleared before every
   frame) and the later ones separately.
