# #132 — BEFORE baseline: the delegation UI in a real TUI (main `9d1517a8`)

Captured 2026-10-30 by a separate lane, unchanged worktree. Durable copy of the drivers
and every grid: `.omc/specs/132-live-drivers/baseline/` in the main checkout (untracked;
`/tmp/132-baseline/` is cleared after ~3 days).

Setup: `aelix --agents --provider scripted --model s-1 --permission-mode yolo`, profile
`worker` (scripted `s-1`, tools `[read, bash, write]`), child replies delayed 0.8 s per
tool call and 1.5 s for the final text, pty + pyte, grid sampled every ~0.3 s. Driver
`run.py <scenario> <cols> <rows>`; scenarios single, parallel (3 tasks: sleeps 6/10/14 s),
chain (2 steps), esc-single, esc-parallel; sizes 80x30, 120x40, 60x30 (+240x40 diagnostic).

## Problems seen

1. A single delegation shows no per-child progress at 80 or 120 columns (status row taken
   by the disclosure, cut with no ellipsis; no transcript progress body).
2. The status row is spent on the disclosure; the per-child / aggregate segment after it
   is pushed off-screen at 60, 80 and 120 columns.
3. The panel's numbers column is ~12 cells at every width (`running · b…`), unreadable even
   at 240 columns because the panel is capped at 78 cells.
4. Updates come only with events: the panel header read `2s` from t≈0.9 to 9.7 s; the
   single segment stayed `bash · 2s` for 12 s while `Working…` ticked every second; fast
   steps (`read`, `write`, `echo`) never appeared.
5. The last child never shows `done` — the panel disappears with the tool's return.
6. Chain row 2 shows the raw template `{previous}`.
7. (transcript, out of scope) The tool header is a raw Python repr cut after the first
   task; the result card shows member 1 only.
8. Esc: ~1.0–1.4 s with no acknowledgement (`Working…`, panel still `running`), then
   `✖ Operation aborted` with no per-child record. No stale row left.
9. (process, #619) `sleep` grandchildren survive Esc, reparented to PID 1.
10. (unrelated) the footer row is cut at the right edge at 80 and 60 columns.

Not measured: a non-yolo parent; an idle baseline without delegation.
