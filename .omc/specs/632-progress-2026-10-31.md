# #632 — the chrome stays on the last rows: progress (2026-10-31)

Issue #632: after an in-flow modal closed (`/model`, `/settings`,
`/scoped-models`, `/extension`, the delegation consent dialog, a tool approval),
the input, status line and footer stayed where the modal had been and the rows
below them were empty. Branch `fix/632-modal-close-anchor`, worktree
`aelix-ai-worktrees/claude-632`. Started on `main` `e19ab55e`; the one commit
now sits on `6bc82811`. ADR: `docs/decisions/0280-the-chrome-stays-on-the-last-rows-once-the-transcript-fills-the-screen.md`
(amends ADR-0159 and ADR-0194). The implementation lane did not approve its
own work: a Codex review, an independent verifier and two delta verifiers did.

Raw runs are not in the repository. They are kept locally only: the design
measurements and prototype patches in `.omc/specs/632-evidence/`, the lane's
pty runs under `/tmp/632/` (`final/`, `final2/`, `final3/`), and the
verifiers' drivers, probes and runs in `.omc/specs/632-verify-probes/`,
`632-verify-r1-probes/` and `632-verify-r2-probes/`, with their reports beside
them. Every TUI run used the real `aelix` in a pty replayed through pyte, a
scripted provider, a scratch HOME and a scratch cwd.

Prototypes, measured on the same grids:

- **A, bottom pin** (chosen): a pad above the chrome takes the spare rows once
  the chrome has reached the bottom; the empty rows become a band above the
  input that the next print fills.
- **A1, an anchor row**: put the chrome back on its pre-modal row. Rejected:
  it missed the approval after a stream (the anchor was taken inside a
  region the stream tail had grown).
- **A-alt, pin plus anchor**: rejected; it left a 10-row band mid-screen in a
  fresh session and needed a CPR wait in `show_modal`.
- **B1, pi's `clearOnShrink`** (clear the screen and the scrollback, replay
  the session): rejected; it fixes only modals that close between turns, costs
  5–7 KB per close and wipes the banner and every non-session line.
- **B2, erase and measure again**: no effect; the erase returns to the app's
  first row, where the chrome already is.

The owner's answers: the band is acceptable; a screen not yet full keeps
today's layout; holding the 2026 bracket for the CPR answer through the
private `_redraw` is acceptable if every failure falls back to today's
behaviour; try to stop the bounce on terminals without 2026; #84's
`clear_on_shrink` stays inert with its help text fixed; include the `clear()`
renderer reset; write ADR-0280.

## Review round 1 (Codex and the verifier) → `576ce57f`

Codex (C1–C5): a CPR answer to a query from before `clear()` re-pinned an
empty screen for good; the reset after a raw ANSI clear drew the prompt twice
on a legacy `Win32Output`; a missing answer bypassed the failure latch; an open
dialog jumped into the band during the hold; the tests answered every CPR at
once. The verifier (F1–F5): no blocking defect; the hold's cost on a slow
link, a tall draft pinning a screen not yet full, a growable slot child
sharing spare rows with the pad, wrong figures in the ADR, the bracket held
while the event loop is busy.

Fixed: the pin waits for a fresh CPR answer after `clear()` or a resize;
`clear()` clears through `Renderer.clear` and writes only the scrollback's
`ESC[3J` raw, and only where the output speaks VT; the settle's wait is bounded
by `_CPR_HOLD_LIMIT` and a missing answer drops the pin; the hold covers the
modal slot; the limit went from 0.5 s to 0.1 s (no chrome row on the turn
after a close, without 2026, at 0.5 s late answers: 1013 → 108 ms); a draft
counts as one row; ADR-0159 asks slot content to size to its content. The
test harness answers CPR at once, late or never. Rebased onto `6bc82811`
(`e3012a44` and then #634 and #627 had landed): the CHANGELOG kept both
entries, the citation lock was re-locked with `--fix`.

Gates: `ruff` and the type gate clean; citations `902 gated, none drifted`;

## Round-1 delta verifier → round 2, `aa97c565`

Two must-fix defects. M1: a resize while a late answer was outstanding turned
the pin off for the session, because the late check read the live queue,
where the resize's own query looked like a missing answer. M2: on a 2026
terminal, an answer later than 0.1 s painted a frame with no chrome row for
the rest of the round trip (102, 510 and 1007 ms per turn at 0.1, 0.5 and
1.2 s), because the settle closed the bracket with the hold still on.

Fixed: the late check reads the queries the settle waited on, and a resize
bumps `_restart_seq`; past the limit the settle ends the hold and repaints
before the bracket closes. Live: the resize at 0.5 s now stays pinned (a later
`/settings` close at 27/29/30 with no empty row, against 14/16/17 with 13);
the 2026 terminal paints no chrome-less frame at any delay. Docs: the reply
delay measured from Enter, the absence past 0.1 s, the `clear()` edge before
the first CPR answer, the byte ranges.

Gates: `ruff` and the type gate clean; citations `902 gated, none drifted`;
`tests/tui/` `3252 passed, 1 warning`; 3.11 and 3.14 `66 passed` each;

## Residuals (ADR-0280 Limits)

- Past a 0.1 s CPR round trip: the chrome is off for about 0.1 s per print
  without 2026, then drawn up in the band for the rest of the round trip; a
  reply whose first text arrives within 0.1 s of Enter shows up to one round
  trip later.
- A resize with a band open paints no chrome row until the next redraw.
- After a resize, a terminal that has stopped answering is caught one print
  later.
- The bracket is held while the event loop is busy (286–341 ms at the first
  prompt of a pinned session).
- A terminal that drops a 2026 bracket within the 0.1 s paints the held frame.
- `clear()` before the session's first CPR answer can arm the old query.
- `Application._redraw` and `Renderer._waiting_for_cpr_futures` are private.
- The pin is sticky until `clear()`; `_note_render` measures the layout on
  every render while unpinned (about 65 µs).
- Surviving mutants, each a guard another covers.
- Not run on a real Windows console; not viewed by eye in iTerm2 or
  Terminal.app (tmux 3.6 confirmed the fix live).
