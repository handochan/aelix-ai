## What was built

- **Grammar (D1, D2)** — `core/resource_filter.py`: pi's entries plus identity;
  minimatch's glob re-implemented (braces and sequences, extglobs, classes,
  `dot: false`, a second `!`, `#`), pinned by 4,955 minimatch 10.2.6 answers;
  `reaches()` keeps a path form inside its own file's scope.
- **Settings (D4)** — `FileSettingsStorage.transact` (sidecar lock first,
  deadline on POSIX and Windows, strict read, `mkstemp` + full write, symlink
  written through), `read` (strict, lock-free); `SettingsManager.edit_list`
  (joins the queue, touches only its key, raises instead of skipping, off the
  event loop), `read_list`, `load_error`, `scope_path`; setters parse strictly
  and write through `transact` off the loop; list getters never split strings.
- **Selection (D3, D5–D7)** — `core/resource_selection.py` (`ResourceItem`,
  `Snapshot.unreadable`, trust as an input, one scope per save, deferred apply
  on the live harness), `core/skill_selection.py` (`SkillCatalog`,
  `effective_skills`, `SkillKind`, `ApplyGate`), `core/prompt_selection.py`;
  `AgentHarness.prepare_system_prompt()` for prepare-then-commit.
- **Wiring** — `cli/entry.py` (`_resolve_skill_tiers`, `_load_skill_catalog`,
  `_load_prompt_catalog`, `_resolve_prompt_template_sources`, launch warnings,
  `/reload` rescan, `_harness_factory` under the gate), `agents/service.py`,
  RPC `settings/get|set` through the strict path.
- **TUI (D11, D12)** — `tui/selection_panel.py`, `/skill` + `/skills`,
  `/skill:<off>` refusal.
- **Docs** — ADR-0279 (+ notes on ADR-0165, 0169, 0196, 0252), guide
  `skills-and-prompts.md`, `project-trust.md`, bundled `writing-skills`, help
  near-miss, CHANGELOG.

4. **Implementation round 2**:
   - M1: `resource_filter._build` keeps round 1's translation node for node
     and `_run` evaluates it as a table of end positions (int bitmasks, one
     sweep from the segment's end): polynomial — about nodes × characters²
     Python steps — instead of exponential (corrected in the finish pass; the
     round-2 text said "nodes × characters").
     A segment with no extglob is one `fnmatch`-style regex (atomic
     `(?>.*?fixed)` groups). Braces: a sequence's size is checked before it
     is generated, an expansion stops past 256 alternatives (32 globs; plain
     alternatives are a set lookup), 512 characters, 16 nested groups —
     refused by `validate_entries` with "is too complex to match: …; it is
     ignored". `realpath` is cached per path.
   - R1: `_segments_match` (memoized) makes a trailing `**` take at least one
     segment and collapses adjacent `**`; `a/../b` is `b` (minimatch's level-1
     optimisation). Corpus 4 (33 patterns × 31 values) added: 5,978 rows.
   - R2: tests for X2b/X2c (AST pin of the launch wiring), X3def, X5b, X8b,
     V3 — each mutation re-applied and seen red, then restored.
   - R3: trust re-asserted inside the builder; `_EditAbandoned` when the call
     was cancelled before the builder ran; a cancel after it lets the write
     land and commits memory from the thread's done-callback; `_write_task`
     re-raises `CancelledError`. Each fix's mutation turns its test red.
   - R5: `storage._replace` retries `PermissionError` on Windows (5→100 ms
     backoff, 2 s budget); tested with a monkeypatched `os.replace`.
   - R6: warning "kept what was applied: <path> is not valid JSON: …";
     loop-gap test with a 60 s holder and relative bounds (`max(gaps) <
     waited / 2`, ≥ 10 ticks); BOM stripped in load and `_parse_scope_text`;
     `_later` snapshot inside the `try`; `<any>` refused when too small.
   - R4 and the same-key-setter edge: ADR Limits only.
   - `tests/agents/test_p2_band_boundaries.py`: the four glob bounds added to
     the product-core cap allowlist with their reason (not delegation caps).

## Residuals

- Different agent directories do not share the sidecar lock (ADR D4).
- The glob is a re-implementation pinned to a corpus, not minimatch itself;
  the remaining random-differential mismatches are extglob corner cases (a
  repeated extglob's dot rule, empty alternatives, an unclosed `[` inside an
  extglob) and empty values (ADR Limits).
- A `!glob` past the limits is refused where minimatch evaluates it.
- `create`/`reload` take the inode lock with no deadline (R4, pre-existing).
- The Windows replace retry is tested by simulation only.
- A same-key setter racing an `edit_list`'s lock wait leaves memory and disk
  apart; nothing in `src/` calls those setters.
- At launch a settings file that did not load contributes no entries (reported
  as a load error); only the panel apply refuses an unreadable snapshot.
- `/agents use` is not under the apply gate and emits no `resources_update`.
- #632 (input anchored high after a modal closes) is another lane's.
- After a terminal shrinks while the panel is open, pyte shows rows of the
  larger frame above the panel (prompt_toolkit's resize redraw; not the panel).
  The round-2 verifier saw the same after shrinking 80x24 to 80x12 and growing
  back: three rows of the old panel stay above the redrawn panel and remain
  after it closes (`511-verify-r2-probes/tui/run80x24/grids/15-*`, `16-*`).
  Believed to be pyte cropping from the top when its screen shrinks; NOT
  checked on a real terminal. No code changed for it (R-e).
